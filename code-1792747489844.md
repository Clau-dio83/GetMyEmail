# Outreach Mailer

A 100% free, open-source cold email engine for Gmail and Google Sheets. Built as a zero-cost alternative to GMass, Mailmeteor, and YAMM.

## Features
- **A/B Split Testing:** Test subject lines and body copy simultaneously (`A1_A` vs `A1_B`).
- **Open Tracking:** Read receipts powered by an Apps Script Web App.
- **Bot & Scanner Safeguards:** Ignores opens within 120 seconds of sending and requires explicit click confirmation for unsubscribes.
- **Email-to-Row Verification:** Cross-references recipient email addresses to prevent data mismatch when rows are sorted.
- **Automated Follow-ups:** Threaded replies on Day 7 and Day 14 unless a reply or bounce is detected.
- **Draft Preview Mode:** Generates draft previews in Gmail before sending.
- **Campaign Analytics:** Auto-generates a reporting tab calculating open and reply rates.

## Setup
1. Open your outreach spreadsheet in Google Sheets.
2. Go to **Extensions > Apps Script** and paste `Code.gs`.
3. Click **Deploy > New deployment**, select **Web app**, set execution to **Me**, and access to **Anyone**.
4. Copy the Web App URL into `CFG.WEB_APP_URL` at the top of `Code.gs` and save.
5. Reload the sheet and use the custom **Outreach** menu to manage sends and follow-ups.
